The Drowned Abbey

At low tide, Captain Orsa Vell brought the party to the doors of the Drowned Abbey. She refused to come further, warning that the tide turns at the second bell. Pressed by Pip, she repeated her riddle: the diamond lies “beneath the tide’s tongue.”
