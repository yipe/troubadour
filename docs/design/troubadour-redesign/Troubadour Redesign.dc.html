Troubadour, redesigned

The app follows the campaign’s rhythm: Home (what’s next, what needs review), Sessions (prepare, play, review, history), Codex (people, places, things), Facts (what’s true and who said it) and Ask. The type, colours and tabs come from DPRcalc. Each player’s colour is their class colour, so a speaker looks the same in the live transcript, the review, the facts and the recap.
