## Build
- Header: eyebrow "Review · Session 12 · Thu Oct 22", serif 22px title, `Progress` "5 of 14 reviewed" (success) with "~4 min left" (about 20 s per open item), `PillGroup` filters (All · Facts · Spellings · New entries, with counts), "Accept all GM-stated (3)" (secondary), and primary "Finish review".
- Body: `TranscriptList mode="static"` of the saved record with `renderAside`. A line with an item gets `bg-accent-soft` and a 3px left border (accent while open, success when accepted); its aside is a `SuggestionCard`:
  - fact: "Fact → Drowned Abbey" + `ConfidenceBadge`; Accept / Edit / Reject. Edit opens an inline textarea plus an About picker (`Command`), then accepts with the edits.
  - update: the old text struck through + the new text; Accept keeps the history.
  - spelling: "Spelling · Fixed in N lines" or "Unknown word"; Accept / Reject, or "Add to codex" for an unknown word (asks for name and type).
  - entry: "New entry · NPC" + its badge; "Add to codex" (primary) / Edit / Reject.
  - `preaccepted`: a "Kept during the session" note, with Accept highlighted.
  - Decided: "✓ Accepted" (success) or "Rejected" (muted), with no actions.
- Filters hide asides that don't match; the lines stay.
- "Accept all GM-stated": every open fact item with `confidence === "gm"`, accepted one after another through the serial write; a progress toast; it stops at the first error and reports it.
- "Finish review": `useMarkReviewed(id)` → `/sessions/:id` + `notify("Session 12 reviewed")`. With open items, confirm first: "N suggestions are still open. They stay in the codex as suggestions."
- Not written up yet: a banner "The write-up is still running" with `JobStatus`, plus the notebook-only items.

## Tests
Counts and progress; each kind's accept and reject send the right body; edit then accept sends the text; Accept all GM-stated runs serially and stops at an error; Finish marks reviewed and navigates.
