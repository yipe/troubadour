## Uses
`useCampaign`, `useSessions`, `useReview(lastId)` (counts), live store (players), `PageHeading`, `Card`, `Badge`, `Button`, `TalkShareBar`, `PlayerAvatar`, `ConfidenceBadge`, `TimestampLink`, `FactText`, `IconTile`.

## Build (`pages/home/`)
- Header: eyebrow = campaign name; a stats line (`N sessions · 38 h 40 m recorded · 214 entries, 389 facts · ~/Obsidian/… vault path` as a mono link that opens Obsidian via `obsidianUrl`); actions "Ask the campaign" (→ `/ask`) and primary "Prepare session N+1" (→ `/sessions/new`). While a session runs, the primary button is "Back to the session" (→ `/sessions/live`).
- Three columns (`1.35fr 1fr 1fr`):
  - **Last session** card: "LAST SESSION" label + "Session 12" accent badge + date and duration on the right; the serif 24px title; the summary paragraph; `TalkShareBar` with legend; buttons "Open transcript" (→ `/sessions/:id/transcript`) and "Previously on… recap" (→ `/sessions/:id/recap`). Below it, the **review banner** (warning tint, `IconTile`): "Session 12 is waiting for review", "6 facts · 5 spellings · 3 new entries, found by the local model", and a warning button "Start review" (→ `/sessions/:id/review`), shown when the last session is written up, not reviewed, and has open review items. While its write-up job runs, show `JobStatus` inline instead. Not written up: "Write up" button (starts `useWriteUp`).
  - **The table**: players with avatar, coloured name, "Alex · GM, 4 NPC voices" / "Sam · Bard 6", voice status (Trained / Learned in play); "Add a player or a guest" → `/settings/players`. Header meta "5 players · voices trained 4/5".
  - **Newest facts**: the 6 newest confirmed facts by source time: text, then a dot + who + "S12 · 1:38:02" `TimestampLink` + `ConfidenceBadge`. "All facts →" links `/facts`.
- Empty states: no sessions yet → the last-session card says "No sessions yet" with "Prepare session 1".

## Tests
Renders the last session's title, summary and session number from fixtures; the review banner shows the counts and hides when reviewed; "Prepare session" shows N+1; newest facts are sorted by source.
