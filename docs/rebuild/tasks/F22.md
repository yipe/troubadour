## Build
- Eyebrow "Troubadour Cloud", subtitle "A backup of each campaign, and the read-only view your players open in a browser. Audio and voiceprints never go up."
- Not connected: an explainer card + "Connect" (S12's `ConnectDialog`).
- Connected, left: **status** (check `IconTile`, "Backed up 4 minutes ago", "The Sunken Crown · 1,212 files · checks again a few minutes after notes change", primary "Back up now", "Open player view ↗"); **What goes up** (rows with icons: Notes, codex entries and facts → Always; Session write-ups and recaps → Always; Transcripts → switch; Audio recordings → Never; Voiceprints → Never); **Main Mac** ("Main Mac: Alex's MacBook Pro (this one)", "Only one Mac should run this campaign. If another Mac backs it up too, the newest backup wins."); **Back up automatically** switch ("A couple of minutes after notes change, at most every 10 minutes. Never during a live session.").
- Right: **Backup history** ("Kept for 90 days"): attempts with a success/danger dot, time, and summary (files changed, or the failure reason), with "Restore…" on successful ones (`ConfirmDialog` → restore that snapshot); **Other campaigns in your cloud** (vaults not open here: name, device, last backup, "Bring to this Mac" with the old confirm text); footer "Signed in as …" + "Disconnect" (danger, confirm).

## Tests
History renders success and failure; Restore sends the snapshot id; Disconnect confirms; the transcripts switch saves options.
