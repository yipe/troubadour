## Build
- `LivePage.tsx`: three columns below the nav: left rail 250px, centre flexible, right 440px (`bg-sunken`). Centre = `<LiveTranscript/>` (F5), right = `<Notebook/>` (F6), plus `<EndSessionDialog/>` (F7). S1 created the stubs with these props; keep them:
  - `LiveTranscript({ highlightT, onPinned })`
  - `Notebook({ onJump(t), tab, onTabChange })`
  - `EndSessionDialog({ open, onOpenChange })`
  The page holds `highlightT` and the notebook `tab`. `Notebook.onJump` sets `highlightT`; `LiveTranscript` scrolls to and highlights that line; `onPinned` switches the notebook to Pins.
- **Left rail** (page-local components):
  - "Session 13" (bold) and the title if set; a status line: dot + "Listening · Jabra Speak 710" in danger, or "Paused · not listening" in warning. Clicking it opens a `Popover` with the live quality rows (port `LiveQualityBox`).
  - Speaker list: for each present player and named voice, a 30px avatar, name, player name, share %, and a 3px share bar. The current speaker (`useSpeaking`) gets `bg-surface`, an inset ring in their colour and the glow shadow.
  - **Pending voice card** (dashed border), for the newest pending voice: "Voice 3 · 4 s heard · who is it?", a sample play button (click only), and quick picks: the top suggestion (`suggested_player_id`) as an accent chip ("Alex as NPC" when it's the GM: assign to the GM and ask for the character), the other present players, and "Later" (hides it until the next new voice). Assigning calls `assignVoice` and `notify("Voice 3 is Maya. Past lines relabeled.")`.
  - "Voices" button → a `Popover` with the full voices list (port `VoicesPanel`: named and pending, assign/unassign, character).
  - Bottom: "Pause"/"Resume" (secondary), "Export" (`DropdownMenu`: Markdown, JSON), "End session" (danger) → `EndSessionDialog`.
- File-replay sessions: port `SessionAudio` (mute toggle, synced to the session clock).
