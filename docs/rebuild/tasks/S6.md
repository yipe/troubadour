## Build
- `JobStatus`: `{ job: CampaignJob, variant: "inline"|"panel", onDismiss? }`. Inline: a spinner + title + a thin `Progress` + percent. Panel: the steps list (pending/running/done), the running step's bar (port how the notes step advances by beats and the page step by its usual length), the live model line ("Reading… 2 m 14 s" with h/m/s, "Writing · 312 tokens · 18 tok/s"), the snippet marquee (a new find every 12 s; stats repeat while the model reads), and an error state with the message and Dismiss.
- `useJob(kind, title?)` → the latest `CampaignJob` of that kind (and title) from the campaign cache, plus `active` and `failed`. It replaces the four inline `Object.values(jobs).filter(...).at(-1)` copies.
- Keep the old timing constants and formulas; move them into `jobs/progress.ts` with tests.
