## Build (`pages/ready/`)
- Header: eyebrow "Session 13 · ready check", subtitle "Three things to confirm before the dice come out. Each one fixes itself if you skip it, it just gets less accurate."
- Left column, three check cards, each with a circle status (✓ success / ! warning):
  1. **Who's here tonight?**: pill toggles per seated player (avatar + name + player); absent ones at 50% opacity with "away tonight"; a dashed "+ Guest" pill that goes to `/settings/players` (adding a guest is a player edit; keep one place for it). "4 of 5 · tap to toggle". Everyone is present by default.
  2. **Microphone**: `MicCard mode="ready"` (device + replay sources).
  3. **Models**: three page-local tiles (`ModelTiles`): Speech to text (Whisper model, Ready/Loading), Voice ("pyannote embeddings", "Ready · 9 known voices"), Notes & answers (LLM model, Ready / "Loading" / "Not running"). Header "All local · Change" (→ `/settings/models`).
- Right column (400px):
  - **Start the session**: "Working title" input (placeholder "Session 13 (the write-up names it)"), switches "Keep the audio recording" and "Fix names as it goes", the consent checkbox "Everyone here knows they're being recorded." (Start is disabled until it's ticked), and a 48px primary "Start session".
  - **Previously on…**: the last write-up's summary in serif 16px, "45 sec read", `ReadAloud`, and an "Edit" button that opens `/sessions/<last>/recap`.
- Start: the old red checks (mic quality poor, models not ready) open a `ConfirmDialog` "Start anyway?". Start calls `useStartSession` with `source`, `title`, `present_player_ids`, `record_audio` and `cleanup`, then navigates to `/sessions/live`. Remember the chosen mic in localStorage (old key `troubadour.mic`; migrate `bard.mic`).
