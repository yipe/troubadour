## Build
`ReadAloud`: `{ sessionId, text?, defaultPlayerId? }`. A button "Read aloud in <name>'s voice" (volume-2 icon) and a small voice picker among players with a built clone (`useClones`). If there are none, it is disabled with a tooltip "Make a voice clone in Settings › Players & voices". Click starts the `read` job (`cloneRead`), shows `JobStatus` inline, then a play/pause control for the finished clip. **No autoplay**: the clip plays only on a click. Keep the "in their words" switch where the old one offered it.

## Tests
No clones → disabled with the tooltip; click → `cloneRead` called; when the job finishes, the clip doesn't play until clicked.
