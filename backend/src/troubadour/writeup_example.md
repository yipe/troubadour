## Last time
The party left Millbrook at first light with the miller's map and two mules, still arguing about whether Odric the toll-keeper could be trusted. Halfway up the Greyfen road they found his toll bridge cut through and Odric himself tied to a post, swearing that a band of goblins under a chief called Snagtooth had taken the bridge chest and his daughter, Wren. Brannoc wanted to go straight after them; Ysolde talked him into first asking Odric what was in the chest, and he admitted it held the village's tax silver as well as his own savings.

They tracked the goblins to a flooded quarry. Pip slipped ahead invisible and counted eleven goblins and one chained ogre the goblins called Mudge. The fight at the quarry gate went badly at first: Mudge broke free, knocked Tamsin unconscious with one blow and nearly threw her into the water, until Ysolde got her back on her feet with a healing word. The turn came when Pip cut the last of Mudge's chains and offered him the mules' supper; the ogre sat down to eat and refused to fight for Snagtooth again. Snagtooth fled up the quarry stairs with the chest, and Brannoc caught him at the top and knocked him out rather than kill him.

Wren was found locked in the goblins' cookhouse, unhurt and furious. The chest held 340 silver and a sealed letter addressed to someone called "the Lantern" in Greyfen, which nobody has opened yet. The party took Snagtooth back to Millbrook as a prisoner, returned the tax silver to the reeve and kept Odric's 60 silver as their reward. Mudge followed them as far as the bridge and stayed there; Odric has agreed to feed him in return for guarding the crossing. The session ended in the Millbrook inn, with Tamsin still bruised and the letter on the table between them.

## Spotlight
- **Brannoc:** Ran down Snagtooth on the quarry stairs and chose to take him alive.
- **Ysolde:** Got the truth about the chest out of Odric, and kept Tamsin alive in the quarry fight.
- **Pip:** Scouted the quarry invisible, then freed Mudge and turned the fight with a mule's supper.
- **Tamsin:** Held the quarry gate alone until Mudge knocked her out; still bruised at the end.

## Highlights
- Odric, tied to his own toll post, admitting the "savings" in the chest were the whole village's tax silver.
- Mudge the ogre sitting down mid-fight to eat the mules' supper and quitting Snagtooth's band.
- Brannoc tackling Snagtooth at the top of the quarry stairs and knocking him out instead of killing him.
- Wren, found locked in the cookhouse, more angry than scared.
- The sealed letter to "the Lantern", still unopened on the inn table.
